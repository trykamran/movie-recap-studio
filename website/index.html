How It Works in 4 Steps

Story & Subtitle Ingestion

AI reads and structures the entire film in seconds

Drop your raw movie subtitle file (.srt) into Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini. The skill parses chronological dialogue beats, detects character motivations and turning points, and suggests 2–3 focused storytelling angles.

Step 02 70%+ retention

Retention Narration Scripting

Engineered for 70%+ audience watch time

The AI writes a transformational script using the proprietary 3-Sentence Curiosity Hook (Curiosity + Extreme Stakes + Dilemma). Every beat maintains a punchy 135–145 wpm cadence with natural suspense connectors.

Step 03 Sub-100ms sync

Audio Calibration & Scene Sync

Dialogue-grounded frame-accurate cut marks

Generate your voiceover with ElevenLabs, Edge-TTS, or OpenAI audio. The agent analyzes actual spoken audio timing and re-reads the movie SRT to output frame-accurate source timestamp ranges for every spoken line.

Once installed, typing /movie-recap-studio or asking the assistant to analyze an SRT file instantly launches the full recap production workflow.
No. The AI Agent Skill is completely standalone. It parses subtitles, writes viral narration, and calculates timestamp cutting ranges directly inside your AI chat. You can manually paste those timestamp ranges in any video editor like Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, or CapCut. However, using the companion Movie Recap Windows or Android app automates the cutting and silence removal in one click.
Movie recaps fall under Fair Use when they are transformational commentary. Movie Recap Studio ensures compliance by: (1) Replacing all original movie audio with your original TTS narration, (2) Generating rapid scene changes that avoid long continuous clips, and (3) Writing analytical, story-driven commentary rather than raw script reproduction.
High-retention video requires fast, energetic pacing. Most TTS engines leave natural 0.5s–1.0s breath pauses between sentences. The Movie Recap companion app scans your TTS audio file, identifies any silence longer than 0.3 seconds, and ripples the timeline to eliminate dead air while keeping video scene transitions perfectly synchronized.
