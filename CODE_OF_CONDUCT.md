# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Focus feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect copyright, privacy, and consent.
- Do not share private files, credentials, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation where necessary.
